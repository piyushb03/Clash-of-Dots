    Tactical Directives

    Objective:

    Be the first player to form a continuous line of four dots horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

    Center Dominance:

    Controlling the center columns grants the greatest number of winning line configurations. Never allow the opponent uncontested center stacks.

    Double Threats (Forks):

    Create board states where you threaten two separate 4-in-a-row completions at once. The opponent can only block one, securing your win.

    Tactical Power-Ups:

    • Bomb: Shatters surrounding 3x3 cells and resets gravity.
    • Laser: Clears an entire column instantly.
    • Freeze: Locks an opponent column for 1 turn.
